About

I work on proximal and remote sensing for monitoring crops and the stresses that affect them, especially plant diseases. I acquire, process and analyse multispectral and hyperspectral imagery from UAVs, as well as field and laboratory spectral data, to characterise plant traits and detect diseases early. My current research combines optical sensing and artificial intelligence for high-throughput plant phenotyping and digital plant pathology.

I am an agronomic engineer, with an MSc in soil and plant nutrition and a PhD in geo-information science and remote sensing from Wageningen University & Research. I develop open and reproducible workflows for sensing data in Python and R, aimed at practical use in agriculture. I work in Portuguese, English and French.

Research themes

Plant disease detection

Early detection and severity assessment of plant diseases from spectral data and UAV imagery: late blight and blackleg in potato, Ceratocystis wilt in eucalyptus.

Publications on this theme

UAV-based remote sensing

Acquisition and preprocessing of multispectral and hyperspectral UAV imagery, from radiometric correction to plant traits, including the structure of the canopy from LiDAR and photogrammetry.

Publications on this theme

Crop monitoring

Retrieval of crop and grassland traits, such as biomass and nitrogen content, from UAV and Sentinel-2 data, including how to transfer calibrated models to new conditions with few new samples.

Publications on this theme

Earlier work: soil spectroscopy, from laboratory and on-the-go measurements to imaging spectroscopy and satellite images, for soil texture, properties and salinity (publications).

MicaSense radiometric correction

Radiometric correction of MicaSense multispectral images, from raw values to radiance or reflectance, with the reference panel and optional DLS correction. User interface, command line and Python library.
